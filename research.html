Uppsala University · 2025

GFlow-Pruning: GFlowNets for Single-Cell Lineage Inference

Grew out of a proposal applying GFlowNets to probabilistic cell-lineage reconstruction from single-cell transcriptomic data. Combines GFlowNets with structural constraints so the model samples biologically valid lineage trees proportional to a gene-expression reward, reaching 100% structural validity and zero-shot transfer in evaluation.
